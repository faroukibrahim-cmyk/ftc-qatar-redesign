02 · How a cold facility works

From the dock to the plant room.

Every temperature-controlled building is a chain. We engineer every link, so the cold you pay for stays where your product is.

  1. 01
    At the dock
    Dock levellers, shelters, rapid and sectional doors that seal the cold in.
  2. 02
    In the cold room
    Insulated panels, cold-storage doors, evaporators and hygienic finishes.
  3. 03
    In the plant room
    Central power racks, condensers, ice banks and industrial ammonia chillers.
  4. 04
    Across the building
    Air conditioning, air handling, ventilation, fire-rated and acoustic envelope.
  5. 05
    For its whole life
    Spare parts on the shelf in Doha and maintenance teams on call 24/7.

08 · Eighty years of cold

It started with a showcase.

  1. 1946

    Antoine Farjallah founds Iceberg, building refrigerated showcases.

  2. 1975

    Farjallah Trading Company is set up for electro-mechanical refrigeration.

  3. 2008

    FTC Qatar is founded in partnership with Abu Issa Holding.

  4. 2017

    Building Envelope Solutions (BES) opens in Qatar.

  5. 2023

    New company-owned GCC headquarters in Doha, 15 years in Qatar.

  6. 2024

    FTC Arabia opens in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
